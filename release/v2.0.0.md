# GAMEBOY LAB v2.0.0 — Hardware Audit

v2.0.0 is a full emulation-engine release: CPU bus timing, interrupts, timers,
DMA, PPU, APU, cartridges, RTC, serial, infrared, browser scheduling, and audio
delivery were audited together against primary hardware documentation and
independent test ROMs.

## Measured improvements over v1.3.0

- Mooneye production-model acceptance rose from **33/66 to 66/66**:
  **+50 percentage points** and **+100% more passing tests**.
- Mooneye emulator-only rose from **14/28 to 28/28**:
  **+50 percentage points** and **+100% more passing tests**.
- Median DMG core throughput rose from **825.99 to 1,100.66 frames/s**:
  **+33.25%**.
- Median GBC core throughput rose from **893.99 to 1,053.37 frames/s**:
  **+17.83%**.
- Final v2.0.0 results also include **12/12 Mooneye PPU**, **26/26 Blargg
  APU**, **22/22 Blargg CPU/instruction**, and pixel-perfect dmg-acid2 and
  cgb-acid2 reference frames with **0 mismatched pixels**.

Performance figures are seven-trial medians after 400 warm-up frames, measuring
1,000 frames per trial. They measure available core throughput; emulation still
runs at the Game Boy hardware cadence of approximately 59.7275 Hz.

## Accuracy work

- M-cycle CPU bus accesses, interrupt cancellation and reprioritisation,
  illegal-opcode lockup, HALT/`EI`, STOP, and double-speed behavior.
- TIMA overflow/reload windows, DIV/TAC glitches, APU/serial divider edges, and
  internal or externally clocked serial transfers.
- Timed OAM DMA startup/restart, bus conflicts, DMG OAM corruption, GDMA/HDMA
  stalls, PPU access windows, and additional CGB registers.
- Event-driven PPU modes, LCD startup, LY/LYC/STAT edge cases, sprite penalties,
  priority behavior, and exact LCD-off frame accounting.
- MBC1 multicarts, MBC2, MBC3 RTC-only and invalid selections, MBC5 rumble
  masking, elapsed-time RTC restoration, serial-link hooks, and CGB infrared
  hooks.
- Hardware-timed pulse, wave, and noise channels; length, envelope, sweep,
  trigger, DAC, wave RAM, LFSR, and NR52 power quirks.

## Audio and latency

- Exact sample-window integration replaces point sampling of the APU waveform.
- The analog coupling coefficient now follows the browser's actual sample rate.
- The dedicated audio thread uses bounded interpolated clock correction rather
  than dropping whole sample blocks.
- Underruns fade cleanly and restart with a ramp; overruns are bounded by the
  selected latency profile.
- The main-thread fallback uses a fixed 512-frame callback, removing a previous
  configuration that guaranteed initial underruns in three of four profiles.
- A 30-second exact-hardware-cadence AudioWorklet test finishes with zero
  underruns and zero overruns.

## Efficiency

Quiet CPU clock domains are batched without skipping observable events. The PPU
advances between hardware events, the APU renders only at register/sequencer
boundaries, and reusable buffers reduce allocation pressure. The scheduler keeps
emulation, audio, and presentation decisions separate so optional frame skipping
never skips CPU, PPU, APU, mapper, timer, input, or save work.

## Scope and evidence

Four Mooneye boot-state ROMs target silicon/firmware revisions different from
the supplied production DMG and CGB ABCDE boot ROMs; they are excluded from the
66-test applicable denominator and documented in `EMULATION_AUDIT.md`. The raw
unfiltered result is 66/70 versus 33/70 in v1.3.0.

This release does not pretend that a finite suite proves every hardware state.
Special cartridges such as MBC6, MBC7 sensors, Game Boy Camera, and HuC
controllers remain unadvertised rather than being partially emulated. The full
methodology, source links, commands, exclusions, and deliberately unchanged
areas are recorded in `EMULATION_AUDIT.md`.
