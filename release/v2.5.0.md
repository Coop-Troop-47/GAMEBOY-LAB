# GAMEBOY LAB v2.5.0 — steadier colour-console sound timing

This is a focused accuracy release. It improves the small timing details that
control CGB volume envelopes while keeping the rest of the emulator and the
single-file workflow unchanged.

## What you should notice

On colour-console games, notes that fade, restart, or change their volume while
playing now follow the hardware's divider rhythm more closely. That makes
short musical envelopes and sound effects—such as a note being retriggered or
quieting during a busy scene—less likely to step at the wrong moment. The Game
Boy clock is still fixed; this does not speed games up or slow them down.

## Measured improvement

The external CGB audio timing set moved from **47/70** passing cases to
**49/70**:

- **+2.86 percentage points** of the suite.
- **+4.26% relative coverage** (two additional cases out of 70).
- **0 new timeouts**.

The practical gain is in envelope edges and divider-triggered volume changes,
not in adding synthetic volume or changing a game's mix. Final frame/CPU
checksums for the normal cartridge smoke tests remain unchanged.

## Verification

- **58/58** project tests pass.
- ESLint passes.

- SameSuite CGB APU is **49/70**, with no timeouts.

The remaining PPU and APU cases cover different hardware revisions and are
